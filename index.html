The Film

A faith that is danced, read and defended

Candomblé is an Afro-Brazilian religion that carries centuries of memory, music and community. This documentary follows two terreiros, the sacred houses where the religion is practiced, and asks what it means to live that faith in two very different places.

In Salvador, on an island in Bahia, the religion is lived with calm. In Itaquera, a favela in the East Zone of São Paulo, the same faith has to face prejudice and persecution. Through dance, the game of búzios and the voices of the people who keep these houses alive, the film puts the two realities side by side.

The main character of the film is Candomblé itself.

What the film explores

Four ways into the story

01

Dance

Movement is a form of prayer. The camera stays close to the dances that open the ceremonies.

02

Búzios

The game of cowrie shells is how guidance is asked for and received inside the terreiro.

03

Community

Followers, elders and families share the work of keeping the house and the tradition alive.

04

Intolerance

Religious intolerance shapes daily life for many practitioners, and the film does not look away from it.

Two terreiros

Same faith, two realities

An island in Salvador

Here the religion is practiced in a more peaceful setting. The rhythm of the house follows the ceremonies, the sea and the people who have gathered there for years.

  • Salvador, Bahia
  • Island setting
  • Filmed in 2024

Itaquera, East Zone

In a favela in the East Zone of the biggest city in Brazil, the same faith lives under pressure. Practitioners face prejudice, and keeping the terreiro open is an act of resistance.

  • São Paulo, SP
  • Itaquera
  • Filmed in 2024

Director's statement

Yasmin Barbosa

I wanted to show Candomblé as the people who live it show it to me. Two terreiros, two cities, and one religion that matters deeply to them and to Brazil. The film follows the dances and the búzios, and it also stays with the difficult part: the intolerance that many practitioners face every day.
